Product Line Profitability & Margin Analysis — Nassau Candy

Unified Mentor Pvt. Ltd. • April – July 2026

Executive Summary

Engineered an end-to-end Machine Learning profitability system based on 10,194 historical order records across 15 retail products and 3 operational divisions. The goal was to eliminate margin blind spots and provide executive leadership with automated threshold alerting.

Key Analytical Findings

  • Pareto 80/20 Rule Identified: 4 out of 15 products accounted for ~80% of net company profits, highlighting high vulnerability to single-product supply disruption.
  • Unsupervised Product Clustering: Implemented K-Means (k=3) clustering with standard scaling to categorize items into Star Performers, Average Volume, and Dead Weight.
  • Critical Margin Outliers: Identified Kazookles operating at an unsustainable 8% gross margin, contrasting sharply with the 5 Wonka Bar variants generating 65–71% gross margin.

Live Streamlit Dashboard Architecture

Delivered a 5-module interactive dashboard utilizing Streamlit and Plotly featuring real-time division filters, dynamic date-range selector, margin variance gauges, and automatic threshold warning flags.
